Washington has replaced a trade-policy rumour with a document. The rates remain proposals, but the countries, legal basis and potential tariff ceilings are now public. That reduces one uncertainty while increasing another: businesses can see the scale of the threat, but not the final product-level cost.

## 1. USTR proposed tariffs across 14 economies

The U.S. Trade Representative determined that manufacturing policies in 14 economies were actionable under Section 301. Proposed ceilings range from **15% to 35%**: 35% for Vietnam, 25% for India, 20% for China and Singapore, 18% for five economies and 15% for five. Exemptions, existing duties, public comments and negotiations can change the product-level result.

The Nasdaq fell as much as 4.6% on Wednesday before closing 0.62% lower. The Dow reversed an intraday loss of more than 1,100 points to finish 0.33% higher, while the U.S. 10-year Treasury yield fell six basis points to 4.84%. These moves show uncertainty rather than a settled estimate of the tariff cost.

Nigeria is outside the 14 investigations, but it is exposed through suppliers. Machinery, electronics, vehicles and intermediate goods routed through affected economies could become dearer or be redirected toward other markets. The naira effect will depend on whether cheaper diverted imports outweigh weaker global risk appetite and higher dollar-funding costs.

**Sources:** [USTR, 25 November](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural); [Reuters, 25 November](https://www.reuters.com/business/global-markets-risks-analysis-graphic-pix-2026-11-25/).

## 2. Google put workplace agents behind governed access and usage tiers

Google launched Standard and Scaled access tiers for Workspace APIs and Model Context Protocol servers. The no-cost Standard tier allows up to 5,000 MCP requests a day for each project and product. From 1 February 2027, AI agents accessing Workspace data must use Google's MCP interfaces; Scaled-tier billing starts on 1 April, with Gmail, Calendar and Drive MCP requests charged from the first successful request.

The important change is control, not only price. Agent identity, permissions, audit logs, data-loss rules and user confirmation become part of the access route. Nigerian banks, professional firms and public agencies testing agents against email and documents now have a dated migration and cost-planning deadline. Some MCP tools consume several API requests, so a nominal 5,000-call allowance may support fewer completed tasks.

**Sources:** [Google for Developers, 24 November](https://developers.google.com/workspace/guides/quota-usage-tiers); [Google Workspace, 24 November](https://workspace.google.com/blog/product-announcements/built-for-trust-and-control-new-ai-agent-protections-and-developer-tools-in-google-workspace).

## 3. AfDB and EBRD widened their African SME-finance partnership

The two development banks agreed to combine financing, risk-sharing and advisory support for smaller businesses in Côte d'Ivoire and across Africa. The announcement contains no new facility size or disbursement timetable, so the immediate value is institutional alignment rather than fresh cash.

One number shows the available domestic capacity. Côte d'Ivoire's social-security fund said its financial portfolio had grown from CFA94 billion in 2012 to CFA2 trillion today, while company investments rose from CFA11 billion to more than CFA500 billion. Nigeria's pension and insurance pools are larger. The transmission test is whether guarantees, market rules and investable vehicles can move more of that capital into productive firms without weakening prudential standards.

**Source:** [African Development Bank, 25 November](https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-ebrd-step-partnership-98938).

## 4. Tinubu joined a security directive to an election rally

In Maiduguri, President Bola Tinubu ordered security agencies to intensify operations against fleeing insurgents and said intelligence efforts would seek the rescue of abducted Askira Uba schoolchildren. He delivered the directive during an APC rally for the January 2027 election.

Security, reconstruction and campaigning now share the same stage in the North-East. The claim of recovery requires public evidence: rescues, sustained reductions in attacks, safe roads, school attendance and functioning local markets. Operational detail was not published, and the abducted children remain the immediate test.

**Source:** [State House, 25 November](https://statehouse.gov.ng/in-maiduguri-president-tinubu-assures-citizens-of-sustained-peace-security-orders-army-to-increase-onslaught-on-fleeing-terrorists/).

## 5. Nigeria lost Pete Edochie at 79

Veteran actor and broadcaster Pete Edochie died in Enugu on Wednesday. His portrayal of Okonkwo in *Things Fall Apart* helped place Nigerian literature, language and performance before a mass African audience long before Nollywood became a global commercial label.

The loss is personal for his family and colleagues, and institutional for Nigerian culture. Preservation now matters: accessible archives, rights management, restored recordings and serious training would carry more of his work forward than ceremonial praise alone.

**Source:** [Federal Ministry of Information and National Orientation, 25 November](https://fmino.gov.ng/minister-musawa-mourns-pete-edochie-nigeria-loses-an-iroko-and-giant-of-culture/).

## Three numbers worth remembering

- **14:** economies covered by USTR's Section 301 determinations.
- **5,000:** daily MCP requests per project and product in Google's Standard tier.
- **CFA2 trillion:** the current financial portfolio reported by Côte d'Ivoire's social-security fund.

## What could change everything?

Final U.S. tariff schedules could narrow or widen the product shock. Published Google prices and real agent workloads will determine the cost of governed access. A funded AfDB-EBRD programme would turn cooperation into measurable lending. In Borno, verified rescues and durable civilian security matter more than rally claims.
