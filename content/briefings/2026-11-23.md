The retreat in AI-linked shares became a policy event in Asia. It sharpened the question beneath the sector's valuations: how quickly will debt-funded data centres produce cash returns?

## 1. A 10% Nasdaq fall tested the financing case for AI

The Nasdaq lost **10%** last week, its largest weekly fall since March 2020. Taiwan recorded its steepest weekly decline since 1990, while South Korea's KOSPI fell about 5% on Monday. Taiwan prohibited shorting previous-session fallers and raised the short-sale margin from 130% to 150%. China eased margin-financing requirements, and South Korea said stabilization funds were available. Early Monday, Nasdaq 100 futures rose about 3%.

Investors are assessing whether heavily borrowed infrastructure spending can generate revenue before refinancing costs and depreciation reduce returns. Dell's results on Tuesday and US personal-consumption-expenditure data on Wednesday will add evidence.

For Nigeria, the immediate transmission runs through global risk appetite, foreign funding costs and exchange-rate pressure. Longer term, more demanding project economics could slow financing for data centres and digital infrastructure.

**Sources:** [Reuters, 23 November](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-11-22/); [Taiwan Financial Supervisory Commission via CNA, 21 November](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611210013); [Taiwan Ministry of Finance, 6 November](https://www.mof.gov.tw/eng/singlehtml/6727?cntId=8c25356e7310486a99b5845d1f8b6840).

## 2. A suspected drone attack struck a Russian vessel in Benghazi

Libya's eastern government said a preliminary investigation indicated that a drone probably caused an explosion aboard a Russian vessel at Benghazi naval base. Ten people suffered minor injuries. Satellite provider Vantor identified the vessel as the Russian Navy corvette *Merkury* and said it appeared mostly submerged. No party had claimed responsibility by the cut-off.

The attack extends maritime risk into a Libyan naval base, although attribution remains unresolved. A sustained campaign around North African ports would affect security planning, insurance and Russia's military logistics in Africa. Nigeria's direct trade exposure is limited, but wider threats to port infrastructure would raise regional shipping risk.

**Sources:** [Libyan News Agency, 22 November](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=370568&lang=ar); [Libyan Naval General Staff via LANA, 22 November](https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=370606&lang=en); [Reuters, 22 November](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/libyas-eastern-government-suspects-drone-attack-caused-explosion-russian-ship-2026-11-22/).

## 3. Ghana aligned its policy rate with the rate it was operating

The Bank of Ghana cut its stated policy rate from **14% to 11%**, replaced its 14-day open-market instrument with a seven-day instrument and narrowed the rate corridor from plus or minus 400 basis points to 100. Market rates had already moved below the announced rate, so the package repairs the link between the headline signal and liquidity management.

The bank expects inflation to return gradually to target but identifies oil, El Niño, food supply and utility tariffs as risks. The change gives West African central banks an example of framework repair. It may alter relative demand for regional fixed-income assets, without implying a change in the CBN's stance.

**Source:** [Bank of Ghana Monetary Policy Committee, 19 November](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/MPC-Press-Release-November-2026.pdf).

## 4. Nigeria took the chair of Africa's asset-recovery forum

ICPC chairman Musa Adamu Aliyu was elected chair of the African Asset Recovery Practitioners Forum at its first General Assembly in Abuja. Members adopted a 2027 to 2029 work plan and committed to faster tracing, freezing, confiscation and repatriation of corruption proceeds.

Nigeria now has an agenda-setting role where assets routinely cross jurisdictions. The statements provide no recovery values, country targets, deadlines or public reporting framework. Completed returns, case duration and transparent use of repatriated funds will measure delivery.

**Sources:** [ICPC, 20 November](https://icpc.gov.ng/icpc-chairman-dr-musa-aliyu-elected-substantive-chairperson-of-african-asset-recovery-forum/); [State House, 22 November](https://statehouse.gov.ng/president-tinubu-congratulates-icpc-chairman-musa-aliyu-on-election-as-substantive-chairman-of-african-asset-recovery-forum/).

## 5. OpenAI proposed a common limit for frontier-model development

OpenAI's chief scientist proposed using cross-entropy loss as a shared measure for model-development limits. Developers would avoid specified thresholds until monitoring reaches defined milestones. Differing training data complicate cross-lab comparison, and the measure remains detached from many practical evaluations.

This company proposal becomes useful only if measurement is auditable and developers accept the same constraints. Nigerian regulators and buyers would gain from comparable safety evidence, but still need capacity to test vendor claims.

**Source:** [OpenAI, 22 November](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/).

## Three numbers worth remembering

- **10%:** the Nasdaq's fall last week.
- **150%:** Taiwan's new short-sale margin.
- **11%:** Ghana's aligned monetary-policy rate.

## What could change everything?

US inflation data or weak technology earnings could extend the equity repricing. Verified attribution for the Benghazi attack could widen the geopolitical response. Ghana's new corridor will be tested by actual money-market rates. Published recovery values would turn the African forum's work plan into measurable enforcement. A second major AI developer accepting a common threshold would move OpenAI's proposal toward a usable standard.
