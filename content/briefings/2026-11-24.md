Three maritime pressure points are now feeding the same humanitarian problem. The World Food Programme says disruption around Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb and the Black Sea is raising the cost of food, fertilizer, fuel and transport while aid budgets are already constrained.

## 1. Trade disruption could remove food aid from 7.3 million people

WFP estimates that present conditions would add **$727 million** to its costs by mid-2027, putting assistance for 7.3 million people at risk. About 30% of global fertilizer supplies pass through Hormuz. By mid-October, 63% fewer wheat and maize vessels were moving through the Bosphorus than a year earlier. WFP says about 10 million tonnes of Russian and Ukrainian wheat may fail to reach world markets this year, enough to feed an estimated 44 million people.

The physical supply exists, but conflict and unsafe shipping restrict movement. More than 300 attacks on the Black Sea route have killed at least 40 people this year, according to WFP. The agency already plans to reduce the number of people it assists from 110 million in 2026 to 91 million in 2027.

Nigeria imports fertilizer inputs, wheat and fuel-linked logistics services. Higher landed costs can reach bread, farming and public relief budgets even without a domestic harvest failure. A protected Black Sea grain arrangement covering Russian and Ukrainian exports would reduce the pressure.

**Source:** [World Food Programme, 23 November](https://www.wfp.org/news/wfp-executive-directors-briefing-un-security-council-ukraine-remarks-delivered).

## 2. Oil fell to a three-month low despite continuing attacks

Brent settled **3.46% lower at $89.37** a barrel, its lowest close since 31 August. Higher Middle East supply and the restart of Libya's Sharara pipeline outweighed attacks on shipping, refineries and Russian energy infrastructure. The Libyan interruption had threatened about 350,000 barrels a day before pumping resumed after roughly ten hours. A separate Hamada pipeline remained closed, removing about 15,000 barrels a day.

For Nigeria, $89 oil remains high in absolute terms, but the direction matters for federation receipts, upstream cash flow and the fiscal value of each exported barrel. OPEC+ meets on 29 November to decide its 2027 production policy. Further supply growth would reinforce the downside; renewed disruption around Hormuz would reverse it quickly.

**Sources:** [Reuters, 23 November](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-fall-amid-equity-selloff-though-iran-war-attacks-limit-losses-2026-11-23/); [Libya National Oil Corporation, 23 November](https://noc.ly/en/national-oil-corporation-statement-on-the-closure-of-a-valve-on-the-sharara-oil-pipeline-by-an-outlaw-group-resulting-in-a-loss-of-350000-barrels-per-day-amid-the-continued-closure-of-the-hamada-pi/); [OPEC, 1 November](https://www.opec.org/pr-detail/620-1-november-2026.html).

## 3. ECOWAS put a 2027–2030 framework behind the ECO

West African finance ministers and central-bank governors examined a phased ECO agenda for 2027 to 2030. The work now includes choosing hosts and governance arrangements for a future Central Bank of West Africa, a regional payment and settlement system, and a solidarity and stabilization fund.

This is preparatory work, not a launch decision. The ministerial conclusions still require consideration by the Presidential Task Force, and ECOWAS published no participating-country sequence or conversion rules. Nigeria's size makes its monetary and fiscal choices central. Businesses need the operating design for reserves, settlement, convertibility and bank supervision before they can price any reduction in cross-border friction.

**Source:** [ECOWAS, 23 November](https://www.ecowas.int/single-currency-ecowas-calls-for-decisive-action-to-ensure-a-phased-launch-of-the-eco-from-2027/).

## 4. GPT-6.2 reached OpenAI's critical cyber threshold

OpenAI began rolling GPT-6.2 into ChatGPT for paid individual and workplace plans. Its system card classifies the model at the company's **critical cybersecurity threshold**, and at the high threshold for biological, chemical and AI self-improvement capabilities. OpenAI reports stronger image understanding, difficult web research and instruction following, alongside safeguards used for other GPT-6.2 deployments.

The classification describes capability under OpenAI's framework, rather than a reported breach. It raises the standard for access controls, monitoring and independent evaluation. Nigerian banks, telecoms and public agencies adopting advanced models will need procurement tests that cover misuse and incident response, as well as productivity.

**Sources:** [OpenAI release notes, 23 November](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes); [OpenAI system card, 23 November](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-2-chatgpt/alignment).

## Three numbers worth remembering

- **7.3 million:** people whose WFP assistance could be put at risk.
- **$89.37:** Monday's Brent settlement price per barrel.
- **2027:** ECOWAS's proposed start for a phased ECO launch.

## What could change everything?

A protected Black Sea arrangement would restore grain flows. Escalation around Hormuz would raise both food-input and oil costs. The ECO moves from aspiration to execution when leaders publish the participating sequence, legal framework and settlement rules. Independent cyber evaluations will test whether GPT-6.2's safeguards match its classified capability.
