NVIDIA has begun its first chief-executive search since the company was founded. Jensen Huang's immediate departure transfers responsibility for the central supplier to the AI build-out just five days before quarterly results. The products still ship; the concentration of strategic authority has changed.

## 1. NVIDIA enters the post-Huang era

NVIDIA said Huang resigned as president, chief executive and director on 12 November after 33 years. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is interim CEO and will keep the finance role. The board appointed Harvey Jones as its first chair and opened an internal and external search for a permanent successor.

The board says customer commitments, operations and the product roadmap will continue. Investors now need evidence: management continuity at the 17 November results, retention of technical leaders and a clear allocation of authority between the interim chief, the new chair and the eventual successor. For Nigeria, NVIDIA's chips sit behind imported cloud capacity and planned local data centres. Any change in delivery priorities, pricing or partner strategy reaches Nigerian operators through dollar capital costs and access to compute.

**Source:** [NVIDIA leadership announcement, 12 November](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/).

## 2. Nigeria sets 12 actions for the final year of its health plan

Nigeria's Joint Annual Review agreed 12 priority actions for 2027. They include tighter Basic Health Care Provision Fund accountability, a national health-workforce programme, faster primary-care certification, more emergency services, community monitoring and wider use of DHIS2 as the national reporting platform. The review also called for more domestic financing and support for NAFDAC to reach WHO Maturity Level 4 by 2027.

The completed IMPACT project had **$650 million** of financing. The next phase shifts attention from projects and equipment towards measurable outcomes. The execution test is whether federal releases, state staffing and facility data move together. A checklist of 12 actions will help scrutiny only if each action receives a deadline, owner and public measure.

**Source:** [Federal Ministry of Health and Social Welfare, 12 November](https://health.gov.ng/nigerias-health-reforms-deliver-results-next-phase-to-accelerate-impact-pate/).

## 3. A strike on Nasser Hospital tests Gaza's ceasefire

The UN said an Israeli airstrike on Gaza's Nasser Hospital killed six people, injured 27 and caused extensive damage. Its Middle East coordinator warned that the renewed violence threatens the fragile ceasefire and repeated the obligation to protect medical facilities and personnel. WHO has documented more than 1,000 attacks on health care in Gaza since October 2023.

The immediate issue is human life and access to treatment. Damage to a referral hospital also reduces the system's ability to absorb the next emergency. The next evidence should include an independent account of the target, the warning process and whether surgical services can resume safely.

**Sources:** [United Nations report, 12 November](https://www.un.org/en/six-killed-israeli-airstrike-gaza%E2%80%99s-nasser-hospital); [UN Secretary-General's noon briefing, 12 November](https://www.un.org/sg/fr/node/401528).

## 4. Europe defines its Ukraine peace tests

More than 40 leaders met in Dublin and set three priorities for negotiations: a ceasefire, restored Black Sea navigation and conditions for a peace agreement. European Council President António Costa said concrete progress remains limited and that Russia's conduct over the next weeks will test its willingness to stop the war. EU leaders will consider Ukraine's 2027 financing needs on 26 and 27 November.

For Nigeria, Black Sea access affects grain and fertiliser supply, while a durable settlement would change European energy demand and fiscal priorities. Dublin produced principles rather than an agreement. The measurable next steps are safer shipping, reduced attacks on civilians and a funded 2027 support plan.

**Source:** [European Council summit results, 12 November](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/international-summit/2026/11/12/).

## 5. U.S. refineries accelerate into year-end

U.S. refinery crude inputs rose by 322,000 barrels a day in the week to 6 November, reaching 17.047 million barrels a day. Utilisation increased from 94.5% to **95.2%**. Commercial crude stocks rose by 4.7 million barrels to 414.4 million, while domestic production edged up to 14.013 million barrels a day.

High utilisation supports crude demand, but the inventory build prevents a simple scarcity reading. Nigeria's transmission comes through oil receipts and refined-product costs: strong refinery runs can support crude differentials, while larger stocks restrain the price effect. Watch the next two reports for persistence rather than treating one holiday-delayed release as a trend.

**Sources:** [U.S. EIA inputs and utilisation, 12 November](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/PET_PNP_WIUP_DCU_NUS_W.htm); [U.S. EIA weekly petroleum report, 12 November](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/).

## Three numbers worth remembering

- **33 years:** Jensen Huang's tenure before NVIDIA's first CEO search.
- **12 actions:** Nigeria's agreed health-reform priorities for 2027.
- **95.2%:** U.S. refinery utilisation in the latest EIA week.

## What could change everything?

NVIDIA's 17 November results can show whether the handover changes guidance, customer commitments or the product timetable. Nigeria's health communiqué needs named owners, deadlines and public measures. Verified restoration of Nasser Hospital's surgical capacity would limit the medical damage; further strikes would deepen it. Black Sea navigation would be the first observable move in Europe's three-part peace test. A second week of high U.S. refinery runs without a further crude build would strengthen the oil-demand signal.
