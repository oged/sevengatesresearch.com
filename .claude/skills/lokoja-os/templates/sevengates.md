# Template: Seven Gates Research

For research PDFs, chart packs, investor or reader decks, social cards and Daily Brief visuals. Editorial rules: `modules/seven-gates.md`.

## 1. Palette (locked)

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary identity, framing, values | Ink | `#1A1F24` |
| Keystone accent, rules, emphasis | Brass | `#A67C3D` |
| Brass on dark | Brass Light | `#C69A52` |
| Brass on oxblood, covers, seals | Brass Pale | `#E0B268` |
| Borders, card strokes | Travertine | `#C6BFAE` |
| Soft panels, At a Glance | Parchment | `#F4F0E8` |
| Page and chart ground | Canvas | `#FBF8F1` |
| Covers and seals, rare | Oxblood | `#46160F` |
| Secondary text, muted labels | Slate | `#46504F` |

Deprecated and banned: `#F5F0E6`, `#0B1F33`, `#B08A3E`, `#EDE4D3`, `#64707B`.

Chart rule: warm ivory canvas, Ink or dark navy framing, Brass accents. Green only for genuinely positive economic meaning and red only for genuinely negative. A merely different series is navy, Ink, Brass, Travertine or neutral grey.

## 2. Type

Cinzel 600 for the wordmark and selected display; EB Garamond 400/500 for headlines and prose; Archivo 500 for small caps, labels, navigation and chart text. Fallbacks must render the naira sign; check it in the output.

## 3. Logo

Approved assets only. Do not redraw, stretch, add effects or recolour the keystone away from Brass. Clear space one podium-width. Detailed mark at 40 px and above, small mark below. Wordmark not below 14 px cap height. On the web, text-free mark plus live HTML wordmark.

## 4. PDF and decks

- PDF editions are printed from the final rendered page so that text, charts and the five-year chart are identical.
- Running header: `SEVEN GATES RESEARCH` in Brass caps left; document name right in Slate. Footer: version or date left, page right.
- Covers: Canvas ground, Brass rules, EB Garamond title, a Brass-ruled revision or abstract box, Ink footer band with `INTERESTING FIRST. CORRECT ALWAYS.` in Brass caps.
- Tables: Ink header with white text, Parchment banding, Travertine rules.
- Decks for readers or investors follow `modules/decks.md` with this palette, Canvas ground, message titles and the house disclaimer on the last slide.

## 5. Daily Brief visuals

ViewBox `0 0 1400 760`. Rotate templates (risk ladder, transmission board, countdown, data table); never the same two days running. Fit by arithmetic (Georgia bold about 0.55 x size per character; at 42 px about 10 characters). Check contrast (Slate on Canvas, Ink on Parchment) and preview before publishing.

## 6. Website build values (as built, 2026)

The live site uses warm neutrals chosen by the editor after the first build was judged too dark: ground parchment `#F6F1E4`, bands `#EDE5D3`, cards `#FFFDF7`, links `#7A5A2E` with Oxblood `#46160F` on hover, and a darker green masthead `#26342B` with Brass Light `#C69A52`. Light mode only. Wordmark on two lines: SEVEN GATES in Cinzel 600 (tracking 0.14em) over RESEARCH in Archivo 500 (tracking 0.44em, brass); no tagline in the lockup. The mark is the Etruscan gateway: an arch of seven voussoirs with the seventh a brass keystone, the vault receding to a lit brass core, travertine jambs and podium; files `mark.svg`, `mark-small.svg`, `favicon.svg`; the keystone is never any colour but brass. Article content (charts, At a Glance, PDFs) keeps the brand palette in section 1. Never draw the mark by hand; ask for the files.
