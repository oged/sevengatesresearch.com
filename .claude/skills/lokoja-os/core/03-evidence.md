# Evidence: what we know and how we say it

Style cannot rescue incorrect arithmetic. This file governs every room. House-specific source lists live in the templates and modules.

## 1. Evidence classes

One scheme, with the tags each house uses internally. In published or issued documents the class is carried in the prose ("the audited accounts show", "we estimate", "management says", "by our arithmetic"), not as bracketed tags, except in tables and captions where space is short. A label key sits in the research notes or the method section.

| Class | Meaning | Seven Gates | Operations tag | Consulting tag | Academic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fact | Directly supported by a dated source | Fact | M measured, L allocated | F1 supplied, F2 verified | Cited finding |
| Calculation | Recomputed by us from source data | Reconstruction or Estimate | C | C | Own analysis |
| Basis | Approved design, study or plan | Fact (as a plan) | B basis, P plan | M document assumption | Cited framework |
| Claim | Stated by a company, executive, contractor or interviewee, not proven | Management claim | R report, S statement | M | Self-report, interview data |
| Inference | A reasoned conclusion from known facts | Inference | (in prose) | A analogue, J judgement | Interpretation |
| Opinion | House judgement | Opinion | (the call) | J | Argument |
| Not shown | Not demonstrated from what was reviewed | stated as a gap | ND | ND | Limitation |
| Contradicted | Contradicted by the evidence reviewed | stated plainly | X | X | Disconfirming evidence |

Never convert "not shown" into "contradicted" because the primary data have not arrived. Uncertainty does not become certainty because the sentence looks better in bold.

## 2. Every number is fully specified

A number states, in its sentence or its table header:

- **what it measures** (oil, water, liquid; revenue or profit; nominal or real; naira or dollars);
- **its basis** (nameplate or effective; consolidated or attributable; reported or adjusted; measured, allocated, modelled or planned);
- **its period and averaging** (date or range; average, peak, sustained, point in time);
- **its reference** (a percentage says what it is a percentage of);
- **its scope** (which company, segment, station, country, sample);
- **its date and source** (every price, FX rate and market value carries a date).

Precision without a timestamp is costume jewellery.

## 3. Derived numbers are recomputed

Any number that is calculated rather than quoted (a conversion, multiple, yield, per-share value, growth rate, sum, share of total) is recomputed from its stated inputs at the final proof. A transcribed intermediate is not a check. When a number appears in several places (summary, body, chart, table, PDF, slide), all instances change in the same edit. Round parts by largest remainder so that they add to the rounded total shown elsewhere.

Matched dates: for currency translation, pair each price with the FX rate of the same date and name the FX source. Do not pair a year-end price with an annual-average rate and call the result precise.

## 4. Claim discipline

- **Scope every conclusion to its basis.** "On the operator's capacity case a fifth line has no use after July 2030" is supportable; "a fifth line is not justified" may not be, if the model caps the lines.
- **Say what the evidence cannot show.** A constrained profile hides demand above the constraint; a short trial does not prove a sustained rate; an allocated rate is not measured; a survey shows what people say, not what they do.
- **Upper bounds and estimates are labelled in the sentence**, not only in a footnote.
- **Exact dates and limits**, not rounded ones, when the data hold them.
- **No inflation words**: significant, major, substantial, clearly, fully, all, always, never, unless a number or definition backs them. Use "would" or "is expected to" for anything that depends on an unconfirmed assumption.
- **Name the condition behind every yes.** "Two lines recover the volume if the new train is in service by the first quarter" is a claim; "two lines recover the volume" is a hope.
- **Bad news is stated as plainly as good news**, in the same place and the same type size.
- **Attribute unevidenced inputs.** A figure supplied without a source is written as the supplier's ("per Ops", "according to the sponsor") or as a scenario input, and flagged internally if it drives the conclusion.
- **Field and survey data are not experiments.** Before claiming a cause from operational records or observational data, check selection bias, stratify, consider a placebo or control comparison, and name confounders.

## 5. Sources

General hierarchy, adjusted by the house:

1. Primary records: regulator, exchange and government documents; raw data and executed documents; audited accounts; measured plant data; original datasets; the primary text of a book or the recording of a song.
2. Company results, presentations, transaction documents, approved studies and plans.
3. Transcripts and direct statements.
4. Peer-reviewed literature and recognised standards.
5. Credible wire services and high-quality media.
6. Specialist sources with transparent method.
7. Market-data aggregators, clearly labelled.
8. Social media as a lead, never as proof.

AI output is never a source. A tweet may reveal a story; it does not settle one. Do not recycle an old article, an earlier draft or a previous house view as evidence for a current fact.

For policy, distinguish proposal, issued rule, effective date, implementation deadline and enforcement. For transactions, distinguish rumour, announced intention, signed agreement, shareholder approval, regulatory approval, completion and integration.

## 6. Conflicts

When two sources disagree, show both values with their sources and say which governs and why. Never resolve silently and never average. Use the higher source, and record the discrepancy and the unused figure in the notes so that a reader who finds the other number elsewhere finds the explanation here.

## 7. Verification exceptions

An item that cannot be re-opened at the cut-off may be used only if:

- it is not load-bearing for the rating, the recommendation or the main argument;
- it is described as it is known ("as recorded in the research record", "as given by a broker page"), never as verified;
- it is listed under "references to check" in the notes and in the ledger or log;
- it is flagged on any chart or table where it appears;
- it has an owner and a date by which it is resolved or corrected.

A load-bearing figure is verified, or the piece waits.

## 8. Quotation provenance

A quotation is not publication-ready until its wording is checked against a primary source: the book, the transcript, the liner notes, the recording or the official text. Not a quotation website, not memory, and not wording supplied by a collaborator or an earlier draft. Famous quotations deserve the most suspicion because misattribution is common. If provenance cannot be fixed, paraphrase and attribute, or name the doubt in the text ("a line usually credited to Keynes, though the record is thin").

Copyright: books and songs still in copyright are paraphrased or quoted briefly. Lyrics are limited to about 25 words in a quotation box, credited to the songwriters, only when the editor wants the lyric itself in the piece and it does analytical work; the rest is paraphrased. This is the practice settled on Chellarams in October 2026, when the editor asked for longer lyric blocks: four lines of Genesis (about 20 words) and two of Hotel California (about 22 words) were quoted and the rest paraphrased. The same ceiling applies to prose still in copyright. Every such use is recorded in the quote bank with its verification status. Unverified wording is never presented as checked.

Do not invent dialogue for real people, future figures or hypothetical figures. Composite scenes are labelled ILLUSTRATION.

## 9. Litigation, allegations and people

Say what the document is. A procedural ruling proves that a dispute exists, not a finding on the merits. Name the court, the date and the stage. Do not let a neighbouring sentence imply a result the document does not contain. Allegations, regulatory action, ownership, transactions, safety incidents and personal conduct carry an inline link to the primary source at the claim.

## 10. Current information

Anything that changes (prices, rates, office-holders, regulation, project status, licence status) is checked against a current source and dated. State the data cut-off. If live data cannot be reached, say so and give the date of the last verified value.
