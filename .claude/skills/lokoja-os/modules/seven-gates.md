# Seven Gates Research

Independent research on companies, markets and power. Nigerian and African equities, macro and market structure. House voice: the Lokoja Contrarian. No individual author is named.

This module carries the operating rules of the master standard (v6.4 plus the 31 October 2026 retirements, which together form v6.6 for editorial purposes). The website repository keeps the long-form standard (`docs/editorial-standard.md`) and the ledger (`docs/repetition-ledger.md`); where they are newer than this file, they win on publication mechanics, and this file wins on craft.

## 1. Identity

The seven gates are research disciplines, not compulsory headings: ownership and governance; business economics; financial integrity; capital allocation; competitive endurance; valuation and expected return; downside, catalysts and portfolio fit. A full underwrite tests all seven somewhere.

Always separate asset, business, management, balance sheet and security.

Brand palette (locked, also for Daily Brief visuals): Ink `#1A1F24`, Brass `#A67C3D`, Brass Light `#C69A52`, Brass Pale `#E0B268`, Travertine `#C6BFAE`, Parchment `#F4F0E8`, Canvas `#FBF8F1`, Oxblood `#46160F`, Slate `#46504F`. Deprecated values that must not appear: `#F5F0E6`, `#0B1F33`, `#B08A3E`, `#EDE4D3`, `#64707B`. Type: Cinzel 600 (wordmark), EB Garamond 400/500 (headlines and prose), Archivo 500 (labels, charts). Green and red only for genuinely good and bad economic meaning. Full spec: `templates/sevengates.md`.

## 2. The article package

1. **Kicker**: `SEVEN GATES RESEARCH · SECTION · REGION`.
2. **Headline**: an argument, tension or memorable fact; specific, compact, serious enough to age. No recycled constructions ("A tale of two", "X stands at a crossroads", "no longer just a", "naira says, dollar says").
3. **Deck** (the `excerpt`): what changed and why it matters, in one standalone sentence or two.
4. **Byline**: `By The Lokoja Contrarian · 26 November 2026 · Seven Gates Research`, on its own line. The same date in frontmatter, byline, PDF cover and ledger.
5. **Hero**: editorial art, usually AI-generated and labelled as such; specific to the argument; composed for mobile crop; no stock handshakes or skylines; no fake data; any map, route, flag or landmark checked against a real source; opened and confirmed to decode at full size.
6. **At a Glance**: the decision-useful facts (ticker, dated price, market cap, shares, scale, earnings or cash metric, net debt, valuation multiple, yield, view with horizon and review date, fair-value range, entry zone, principal risk, cut-off). Every number reconciled with the body.
7. **Opening**: earns the next 150 words (`core/01-voice.md` section 8).
8. **The early view**: within the first screens, the view, fair-value range, entry or walk-away price, base case, what would make it wrong, confidence. Never printed as "the answer before the sermon".
9. **Body**: argument-led sections with charts that earn their place.
10. **Verdict**, then **Research notes** (label key, reconstructions, conflicts, verification exceptions, references to check), **Sources**, and the standard **Disclaimer** verbatim.

## 3. Publication ladder

| Mode | Length | Use | Visuals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brief | about 750 words, 5 to 7 minutes | Daily Brief | one chart or image when earned |
| Short | 400 to 900 | earnings reaction, valuation update, event, quick note | 0 to 2, plus the five-year chart for a principal listed stock |
| Standard | 900 to 1,800 | company analysis, thematic and policy essays | 2 to 4, plus the five-year chart |
| Long | 1,800 to 4,000, more only when the research needs it | full underwrites, IPOs, macro and historical essays | 3 to 6, plus the five-year chart |
| Deep or quantitative | as the method requires | rules, CAPE, Taylor and McCallum work | as required |

Choose from the material, not ambition. A length outside the range is a decision recorded in the ledger with its reason.

**Feature length.** The editor has accepted feature essays of about 4,600 to 9,200 words when the piece carries both deep history and a full underwrite (Chellarams, The Republic of 30%, Gun to Head). Treat 4,000 to 9,000 words as a Feature mode that needs the editor's acceptance and a ledger note; it is never the default, and a rewrite keeps the source's length unless asked ("leave the length of the wording").

The flows for each type are coverage checklists, not running orders. Four elements keep their position: kicker, title and deck; hero; At a Glance; the early view.

## 4. Research rules that are specific to the house

- **Nigeria return discipline**: show naira and dollar growth and returns, inflation-adjusted return, dividends and withholding tax, FX basis and date, and a benchmark. Use matched observation dates for price and FX; name the FX source.
- **Liquidity and exit**: free float, investable cap, average daily traded value over stated windows (with a median or recent sessions beside any vendor mean), spreads, suspensions, repatriation, and the time and price impact of a realistic position. For thin stocks, show the last session's turnover in naira.
- **SOTP**: declare for each part whether the value is EV, 100 per cent equity or the attributable stake; deduct debt where it sits and once; deduct minorities only where the value still includes them; include central costs; apply holding-company discounts only with a mechanism; reconcile to the per-share value with a consistent diluted count.
- **Valuation**: state date, reference price, currency, share count, EV or equity, debt and lease treatment, normalised basis, scenarios, dividends, horizon and the main uncertainty. Use the method that suits the business. Run the expectations test.
- **Engineering spine**: nameplate against throughput, production against sales against cash, EBITDA against free cash flow, backlog against funded profitable work.

## 5. Ratings

Ratings are expected-return judgements against the dated reference price over a stated horizon.

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Buy | Expected total return comfortably above the required return, with a survivable downside. The price is the reason to act now. |
| Accumulate | Attractive, not urgent; build on weakness or over time. |
| Hold | Fairly priced on the base case. Owning is reasonable; adding is not. |
| Watch | The business interests us; price, disclosure or evidence does not yet support a position. |
| Avoid | Inadequate expected return, unsurvivable downside, or accounts and governance that cannot be trusted at any price. |
| Not rated | Insufficient evidence, a conflict, or a deliberate choice. |

Every rating carries horizon, reference price date, fair value, entry or walk-away price and a review date (default six months or the next reporting event, whichever is first). At review: reaffirm with a dated note, change under section 6, or withdraw to Not rated. A compound label needs a price level for each half. Only the six labels above exist; "Wait", "Watchlist" or "Own with margin of safety" are not house ratings and are converted to the nearest label with levels. Horizons longer than six months (12 to 24 months for illiquid or turnaround stocks) are allowed when stated with the reason.

First-person holdings: a holding the narrator mentions is a disclosure and must be true. If he holds or has just traded the subject security, say so in one plain sentence near At a Glance. A standing disclosure policy remains an open decision.

## 6. Corrections and changes of view

- A wrong published number, date, name, quotation or attribution is corrected in place with a dated correction note at the foot. Never silently edit a figure that carried the argument.
- A rating change is an article: previous view and date; what changed (facts, price, mechanism, or our reading); which assumption failed; what still holds; the new view with levels, horizon and review date; what would falsify it. Distinguish "the facts changed", "the price changed" and "we were wrong".
- Withdrawal moves the rating to Not rated with a dated reason.
- Every correction, clarification, rating change and withdrawal is logged in the corrections record at the foot of the ledger.

## 7. Charts and visuals

- Real data only, rebuilt in the house system. No AI-generated charts, no vendor screenshots, no generic widgets.
- Figure number, insight headline (the analytical fact, not a dataset title, not personified), unit descriptor, plot, direct labels, source note with date, method note for reconstructions.
- Truth rules: label axes and units; nominal or real; currency; comparable periods; honest axes; actual distinguished from estimate; no causation from simultaneous lines; no false precision; never interpolate or forward-fill to complete a layout.
- **Five-year share-price chart** for every principal listed stock in stock research: trailing five years to the latest verified close; first and last dates stated; daily or disclosed weekly sampling; corporate actions adjusted and disclosed; dividends never silently in a price series; "Since listing" for younger listings; common start date for comparisons; matched NGN and USD dates; sparse series plotted as dated observations, counted and disclosed. It ships with the article.
- **Render check** for every figure on the page and in the PDF: loads in full, title not clipped, no label overlaps, readable at 375 px, naira sign renders, numbers equal the text, source note present.
- SVG fit arithmetic: character width is about 0.55 times the font size for Georgia bold and about 0.5 for Arial. Fit the longest string before drawing it. Brief visuals have repeatedly needed post-publication fixes for clipped or crowded labels and low contrast (September 2026; 18, 22, 23 and 26 November 2026). Measure, check contrast, and preview before publishing.

## 8. Website mechanics

- Article: `content/research/<slug>.md`; briefs: `content/briefings/<date>.md`. Files beginning with `_` are ignored.
- Frontmatter: `draft`, `slug` (equals the file name), `date`, `title`, `excerpt`, `readingTime` (recomputed after the last edit), `kicker`, `researchType` (Essay, Note or Report), `category`, optional `ticker` and `region`, `hero`, `heroAlt`, `heroCaption` (with the AI label), `seoTitle`, `ogImage` (the 1200x630 social card).
- Images: `public/images/research/<slug>/`, hero `00-hero-<name>.webp`, social `00-hero-social-1200x630.jpg`, figures `01-...`. Inline SVG is acceptable.
- Body is Markdown plus inline-styled HTML blocks. An HTML block may not contain a blank line. Markdown tables get a mobile scroll wrapper; hand-built tables need one.
- PDF edition: printed from the final rendered page, published at `public/downloads/<slug>.pdf`, shipped with the article, reprinted after any later edit.
- Pushing to `main` deploys to production through Vercel (team `ogeds-projects`, project `sevengatesresearch-com`). Every push is a publication. One commit carries article, assets, PDF and ledger entry. Messages: `Publish research: <title>`, `Publish <d Month> Daily Brief`, or a plain description for fixes.
- `npm run validate` checks Daily Briefs only. Research articles are checked by the lint (`tools/lokoja_lint.py`) and the gates below until a research validator exists. `next build` may fail where Google Fonts is unreachable; a font-fetch error is the environment, anything else is the content.
- After pushing: wait for READY, load the live page, check hero, figures, tables, disclaimer and PDF. Where the sandbox proxy blocks the live domain, verify through the Vercel deployment tools and say exactly what was and was not checked. The research index may be cached; give the editor the direct link.
- **Superseding a piece**: when a new version replaces a published one, replace rather than duplicate: retire the old page with 301 redirects for the page, hero and PDF, and add a dated correction note.
- **Cross-link the house's own research** where a company or theme has been covered before (link GTCO and MTN Nigeria pages when they are mentioned).
- **Source links**: strip tracking parameters (`utm_*`, LinkedIn trackers). Link to the publisher's page, not an aggregator, and flag any URL that could not be opened. If the network blocked a source (exchange, central bank, vendor price pages), say so; never imply verification that did not happen.
- **Caption truth**: a caption that names a colour, a line or a marker must match the chart. Text inside an AI image (labels, rates, ratings, names, dates) must not contradict the article; if it does, regenerate or caption the image as decorative and remove the text.
- **Interpreting commands**: the editor often sends terse commands from a phone ("Publish", "Review, publish", "Give me the pdf"), and voice-to-text can garble them ("Punish gun to head" meant publish). Act on the evident intent; confirm only when the action is destructive or ambiguous between two different outcomes.

## 9. Gates

**G0.** The current standard has been reviewed against the final artefact.

Hard gate (a no is a stop):

- G1 Current figures verified and derived numbers recomputed; high-risk claims linked inline; exceptions disclosed and not load-bearing.
- G2 Dates, cut-offs, currencies and units explicit and correct.
- G3 Evidence classes visible.
- G4 Every price dated.
- G5 Asset separated from security.
- G6 A clear judgement, with an action where warranted.
- G7 Ratings carry horizon, fair value, entry or walk-away price and review date.
- G8 Ledger and permanent register checked against the final text; this piece's entry written in the same commit.
- G9 Strongest counterargument presented fairly, with disconfirming evidence.
- G10 Major assumptions stated; no false precision.
- G11 Every chart plots real data with axes, units, periods and a source note.
- G12 Green and red used semantically.
- G13 AI art labelled; invented scenes labelled ILLUSTRATION; no false documentary implication.
- G14 No internal framework names, scores or "red team" in public copy.
- G15 Standard disclaimer present; live page and PDF checked as rendered.
- G16 Five-year chart for every principal listed stock, identical on page and PDF, shipped with the article.
- G17 NGN and USD returns built from matched dates, FX source stated, TSR distinguished from price change.
- G18 Liquidity, free float, traded value window and participation assumption stated where exit or size is discussed.
- G19 SOTP bridge declared, debt and minorities deducted once at the right level, reconciled per share.
- G20 Every image, chart and PDF opened and confirmed to decode in full at desktop and 375 px.
- G21 Rewrite or refresh inventory matches except for listed, intended changes.
- G22 One truth across surfaces (date, byline, reading time, price and date, rating, horizon, review date, fair value, cut-off, ticker); mechanical scan clean of em dashes, placeholders, "red team" and deprecated hex.
- G23 Every quotation checked against a primary source; lyrics short, credited and recorded; unverified wording flagged.

Advisory annex: first 150 words compelling; headline argues; deck adds information; tone fits the human stakes; humour serves the analysis; geometry audit passed; scaffolding hidden; length in range or recorded; labels carried in prose; advisory shortfalls disclosed with a plan; opening fresh; no recycled phrase, quotation, guest or ending; vocabulary exact, not ornamental; development drafts' devices not consumed.

Post-publication: P1 deployment READY; P2 live page correct; P3 hero, figures and social card load; P4 tables scroll on a phone, disclaimer present; P5 research card correct; P6 PDF matches the page.

## 10. Daily Brief

About 750 words. Highest-signal item first. For each item: what happened, why it matters for Nigerian prices, positions or policy, and what the market may be missing; dated primary sources. Two dry lines at most. What to watch next with signals and thresholds (not "X would matter more than Y"). Drop low-signal items rather than lengthening.

Operating procedure, frontmatter and visual templates: the `daily-brief` skill, with two overrides from this OS: the brand palette replaces the skill's older palette, and the ledger is appended, never rewritten (`core/05-memory.md` section 6). Rotate the visual template; do not repeat yesterday's. Write "N" for naira in SVG values only if the font test fails; otherwise use ₦ and check it renders.

## 11. The Marcus Daily Intelligence Brief

A separate product: at most 1,500 words; for each item what happened, why it matters, second-order implications and what to watch; keep the DYOR ticker table; drop stories rather than lengthen.
